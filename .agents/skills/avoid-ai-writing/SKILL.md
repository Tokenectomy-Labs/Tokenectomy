# ✍️ Avoid AI Writing: Compact & Authentic Human Developer Voice

A specialized writing standard designed to eradicate the unmistakable formatting, cadence, and vocabulary tells of LLM-generated text. It enforces tight, cohesive, and grounded writing that sounds like an experienced software engineer talking to peers.

## 🛑 The 5 Tells of AI Writing Slop

### 1. Spasi Vertikal Berlebih (Double-Spaced Line Bloat)
* The AI Tell: Placing an empty blank line after every single sentence. It turns every thought into an isolated, melodramatic line break (the "LinkedIn/Twitter bro-post" cadence).
* Human Standard: Group related thoughts into natural, cohesive paragraphs of 2 to 4 sentences. Use line breaks only when shifting topics, not between consecutive sentences.

### 2. The Formulaic Opening & Closing Sandwich
* The AI Tell: Starting with grand rhetorical hooks ("Tired of X?", "In today's fast-paced tech landscape...") followed immediately by a generic call to action ("What do you think? Let me know below! 👇", "Stay tuned for more!").
* Human Standard: Start in media res with the actual technical fact, commit, or measurement. End naturally where the thought finishes without desperate engagement bait.

### 3. AI Buzzword & Adjective Inflation
* The AI Tell: Overusing words like *delve, testament, pivotal, game-changer, seamless, leverage, revolutionize, unleash, tapestry, elevate, crucial, look no further*.
* Human Standard: Use plain nouns and precise active verbs. Say *cut* instead of *dramatically reduce*, *runs* instead of *leverages high-performance capabilities*, and *fixed* instead of *engineered a revolutionary paradigm shift*.

### 4. Artificial Symmetrical Bullet Lists
* The AI Tell: Transforming every paragraph into a 3-item bulleted list where every bullet has a bold title followed by a colon and a 2-sentence explanation.
* Human Standard: Use running prose. Reserve lists strictly for actual checklists, API parameters, or sequential instructions.

### 5. Excessive Self-Congratulatory Enthusiasm
* The AI Tell: Unrestrained optimism ("Excited to share!", "Thrilled to announce!", "Game-changing innovation!") paired with rocket emojis (🚀) and fire emojis (🔥).
* Human Standard: Pragmatic, understated competence. Let the benchmark numbers and code diffs speak for themselves.

## 📐 Formatting Rules for Social Posts (X, Threads, Dev.to)

1. **Compact Density**: Maximum 1 to 2 line breaks per post. No orphan one-liners.
2. **Numbers First**: State the raw reduction, latency, or memory impact immediately.
3. **No Emoji Stacking**: Zero rocket emojis, zero finger pointers. Keep it plain text with at most 1 functional icon if necessary.
4. **Natural Sentence Flow**: Use conjunctions and varied sentence lengths like a human speaking out loud.
